Flappy Race

A fast 2D arcade racing game where every flap matters. Race solo, challenge bots, or jump online for quick multiplayer chaos with items, hazards, and photo-finish leaderboards.

Features

Built for short, wild races

Flappy Race turns simple tap-to-flap movement into a competitive sprint through obstacles, items, and constantly shifting positions.

Simple Controls

Tap to flap, dodge walls, hold your line, and fight gravity all the way to the finish.

Bots Included

Play solo with AI racers when you want a fast match without waiting for other players.

Items and Power-Ups

Grab item boxes and use boosts, lasers, magnets, berries, and other tools to swing the race.

Race Progress

Track where everyone is in real time and push through the final stretch before the leaderboard lands.

Gameplay

One button, a lot of pressure

Timing is everything. Dip too low and the floor gets you. Climb too high and the next wall becomes a problem. Items keep every race noisy, risky, and winnable until the finish line.

Items

Comebacks are part of the race

Item boxes can change the pace fast. Build momentum, protect a lead, or claw back from the back of the pack.

Multiplayer

Race friends or public lobbies

Create a room, join with a code, ready up, and race. Online matches return to setup between rounds, so players can change options, colors, items, and bots before starting again.

  • Ready up system. Players confirm when they are ready so each race starts cleanly.
  • Host controls. Hosts can adjust race settings and start games when everyone is ready.
  • Spectator support. Join late or watch a race without blocking the current match.
  • Bot racers. Fill out races with AI players for more pressure and movement.
