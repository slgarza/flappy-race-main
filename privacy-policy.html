This Privacy Policy explains how Flappy Race for Android handles information related to its users. Flappy Race is a casual game published on Android by SLG Developer. The app does not require users to create an account, sign in, provide an email address, or submit personal details in order to play.

SLG Developer does not sell personal information. SLG Developer does not directly collect personal information such as a legal name, email address, phone number, postal address, precise location, contacts, photos, camera input, microphone input, or payment information through the app. However, the app includes advertising through Google AdMob and optional online multiplayer features. Those features may involve technical data processing by third-party services or transmission of gameplay data, as described below.

No account required You can play without registering or signing in.
Ads The app displays interstitial ads through Google AdMob.
Online multiplayer Multiplayer may transmit session and gameplay data to connect players.
Children's privacy We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

This Privacy Policy applies to the Android version of Flappy Race published by SLG Developer. For privacy questions, parental requests, or support, contact us at support@slgdeveloper.com.

The app does not ask users to provide:

  • Legal name.
  • Email address.
  • Phone number.
  • Postal address.
  • Date of birth.
  • Government identifiers.
  • Contacts from the device.
  • Photos, videos, camera access, or microphone access.
  • Precise device location.
  • In-app payment information.

3. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

The app may store local settings so the game can remember preferences between play sessions. These settings are stored on your device and are typically removed if you clear the app's data or uninstall the app. Local data may include:

  • Sound, music, display, or control preferences.
  • A player nickname selected inside the game.
  • Selected player color or appearance options.
  • Local score, gameplay progress, or game setup options, where applicable.

Please do not use your real name, email address, phone number, or other personal information as your player nickname.

4. Advertising and Google AdMob

Flappy Race displays interstitial ads through Google AdMob. SLG Developer does not control all information that Google or its advertising partners may process to deliver, limit, measure, secure, or improve ads. Depending on the device, region, user consent choices, app store requirements, and Google's policies, Google AdMob may process data such as:

  • Device identifiers or advertising identifiers.
  • IP address and approximate location derived from IP address.
  • Device type, operating system, language, app version, and other technical information.
  • Ad impressions, ad interactions, clicks, and measurement events.
  • Data used for security, fraud prevention, frequency capping, diagnostics, and policy enforcement.

Google's Privacy Policy is available at https://policies.google.com/privacy. Information about Google AdMob is available at https://support.google.com/admob/.

Users may be able to limit ad personalization, reset the advertising ID, or manage ad privacy preferences through Android settings, Google settings, or consent prompts where available.

5. Online Multiplayer

Flappy Race includes optional online multiplayer features. To provide multiplayer, the app may connect to game servers, including official or project-related servers such as jibby.games, user-hosted servers, or other servers selected by the user.

When you use online multiplayer, the app may transmit data necessary to create, find, join, and synchronize a game session. This may include:

  • Player nickname chosen by the user.
  • Player color and appearance choices.
  • Room, match, or join code.
  • Server or room name, if a user creates a game.
  • Ready status, spectator status, host status, and player status.
  • Gameplay events such as movement, jumps, score, death, race time, progress, and match results.
  • Technical network information needed for connectivity, such as IP address, port, latency, and connection data.

Multiplayer data is used to operate online gameplay. Some multiplayer information, such as nickname, color, score, and match results, may be visible to other players in the same game session. Do not use personal information in nicknames or server names.

6. Device Permissions

On Android, the app uses internet access for online multiplayer, ads, and required network connections. The app may also use network state information to check connectivity. The app does not request access to the camera, microphone, contacts, precise location, calendar, SMS, or personal files.

  • Maintain security, prevent abuse, troubleshoot issues, and comply with legal obligations.

We use reasonable measures to limit the information the app directly handles and to reduce privacy risks. However, no method of internet transmission or electronic storage is completely secure. Users should avoid sharing personal information in nicknames, room names, server names, or any text visible to other players.

11. Children's Privacy and COPPA

The Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) protects the personal information of children under 13 in the United States. Flappy Race does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The app does not require accounts, email addresses, legal names, phone numbers, postal addresses, photos, camera access, microphone access, or precise location to play.

Flappy Race is intended for a general audience and is not designed to collect personal information from children. If children use the app, we recommend parent or guardian supervision, especially for online multiplayer and ads. Children should not use real names or share personal information in nicknames, server names, or multiplayer sessions.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe that a child under 13 has provided personal information to us, contact us at support@slgdeveloper.com. We will review the request and take reasonable steps to delete such information if it exists under our control.
